How does it work?

  1. Open any mod page on Nexus Mods and go to the Files tab.
  2. Press Mod manager download or Manual download as you normally would.
  3. The extension resolves the link in the background and skips the wait, the ad panel and the requirement screens.

You must be signed in to your Nexus Mods account. Signed out, Nexus answers with a verification page instead of a download link.
