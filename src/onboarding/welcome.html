NexusMods Bypass is ready!

Download Nexus Mods files and whole collections with less clicking — queued, paced and resumable.

Installed & active Privacy-first Open source

Quick start

  1. 1
    Open any Nexus Mods file page

    Click a download button — the extension prepares the download link and starts the download for you.

  2. 2
    Try a collection

    Open any collection page and use the Collection Downloader deck to fetch every mod with one click.

  3. 3
    Tune it your way

    Click the extension icon for quick toggles, or the settings button on any Nexus page for full settings.

Private by design

No analytics, no accounts, no external servers. Everything stays in your browser.

Something broke?

Every "Report a bug" button copies a full diagnostic report and opens a prefilled GitHub issue.
